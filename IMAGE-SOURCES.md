# Image sources

The studio's own photos live on Instagram (@bloomsofsantabarbara) and The Knot and could not be pulled from this build environment, so the site launches with free-license placeholder photography from Pexels (https://www.pexels.com/license/: free for commercial use, no attribution required). Every file below should be replaced with a Blooms of Santa Barbara photo of the same subject before or soon after launch; `scripts/import-images.mjs` converts a folder of exports into optimized WebP files with the right names.

The founder portrait (`founder.webp`) is a stock photo of a florist, not Sandy; replace it first.
